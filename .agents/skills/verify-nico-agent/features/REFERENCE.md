# Reference: fixtures and handles

What `seed` and `drift` put in a run, and the stable handles a recipe may use. Read the part a feature's recipe needs; `$C help` says how to call each.

## Fixtures

- `seed issues` caches, read now (fresh for a tick, 10 hours here): `verify/fixture#1` "Verify: a Ready issue" (Ready), `#2` "Verify: not labelled", `#3` "Verify: waits on #1" (unresolved Blocker `#1`, no PR), `#4` "Verify: assigned to someone else" (assignee `someone-else`), `#5` "Verify: a Research issue" (Ready, `research`, assigned to the user), `#6` "Verify: stacks on #9 PR" (Stackable on PR #109, head `agent/fixture-9`, which the fixture's origin does not have). The user is `verify-nico`.
- `seed issues second` caches `verify/second#1` "Verify: waits on fixture #1" (unresolved Blocker `verify/fixture#1`, in another Repo), `#2` "Verify: waits on an outside issue" (unresolved Blocker `verify/outside#7` "Verify: an outside Blocker", in no Repo) and `#3` "Verify: waits on fixture #20 installed" (Blocker `verify/fixture#20` "Verify: a closed Blocker", closed at the read: Ready, until verify/fixture is in its Project with an `install` that has not run since).
- `seed dispatch <n> <kind>` records Dispatch `verify/fixture#<n>` "Verify: seeded Dispatch <n>". Use numbers above 6, not 20.
  - `pr`: every step done, in a worktree `worktrees/fixture-<n>` on `agent/fixture-<n>`, pushed to origin, holding `.evidence/<n>/proof.txt`, and its draft PR `https://github.com/verify/fixture/pull/<100+n>` open on the worktree's head in `prs.json`.
  - `asking`: as `pr` without a PR, its Implementer Asking "Should the fixture keep its README? (seeded by verify-nico-agent)".
  - `stacked`: as `pr`, but Stacked on `#9`'s open PR `https://github.com/verify/fixture/pull/109` (head `agent/fixture-9`, on `main`, also in `prs.json`): its Base and its PR's base are `agent/fixture-9`.
  - `restarted`: as `pr`, its Implementer restarted by the loop for two failing Verdicts an hour ago, so the next failing Verdict uses the restarts up.
  - `ruled`: as `pr`, a failing Verdict on its head an hour ago and one `verdict` restart since, its Evidence holding a `rulings.md` that dismisses the one Finding, and no push: with `seed reviewer <n>` (its Implementer idle) the next tick starts a Reviewer on the same head to rule on the Rulings.
- `seed run` records a Run open and blocked since now, as the loop does once herdr reports its agent blocked: `quality-scan` of verify/fixture (agent `quality-scan-fixture`), `morning` (agent `morning`), or `faux-nico <n>` (agent `faux-nico-fixture-<n>`) on the Asking of a Dispatch seeded `asking`. With herdr stubbed, no agent is ever seen ended.
- `seed morning <file>` puts `<file>`'s text in the config dir as `morning.md` (the agent itself is off in a run); `none` takes it away.
- `seed answer <file>` makes the `opencode2` stub answer every headless call with `<file>`'s text, as the wiki model's last message, in a session of its own (`ses_verify_<n>`); with a trail, only the calls whose prompt asks about that part (`## The part: <trail>`, or a correction's `## Corrections to the part: <trail>`). `seed correction <file>` answers each correction (a call with `-s <session>`) instead; `seed answer none` takes every answer away, corrections too. `seed pace <seconds>` has every later call wait that long first, so a build lasts long enough to watch. The stub logs each call after the `OPENCODE_CONFIG_CONTENT` it was given.
- `seed assign answers` makes the `gh` stub answer `gh issue edit <n> --repo <slug> --add-assignee @me` with success, so the Claim and "Assign to me" go through; `none` fails it again, as every other `gh` call fails.
- `seed rate-limit <file> [<bot-file>]` makes the `gh` stub answer `gh api rate_limit` with `<file>`'s JSON, and `ghbot`'s with `<bot-file>`'s (failing, as where ghbot is not installed, without one); `none` fails both again.
- `drift`:
  - `push <n>` commits to the seeded worktree, pushes it to origin and moves the PR's `head_sha`.
  - `stale` dates the cached read 2000-01-01, so the next `status` or `dispatch` asks GitHub.
  - `pause` writes GitHub paused for an hour.
  - `hang` makes every later `gh` call sleep till nico-agent kills it at its deadline (5 s in a run, which sets `NICO_AGENT_DEADLINE_SECS=5`); until cleanup.
  - `below merged|closed` makes PR #109 final; `final <n> merged|closed` makes seeded Dispatch `<n>`'s own PR (`#<100+n>`) final.
  - `base [<file>]` pushes a commit rewriting `README.md`, or `<file>` (made if new), to verify/fixture's `main` and fetches it, printing `{base, file, old, head}`.
  - `tag <tag>` pushes tag `<tag>` to verify/second's origin, on a commit off its `main` adding `lib/greet.sh` and `lib/README.md`, printing `{repo, tag, commit}`. `state` shows each Library's bare clone in the config dir by its tags and worktrees, not its files.

What launch makes: `stubs/` (`gh`, `ghbot`, `herdr`, `notify-send`, `curl`, `opencode2`, each appending its command line to `outside.log` and reaching nothing: `gh` exits 4 as with no login, `herdr` fails with code `verify_stub`, `notify-send` succeeds silently, `curl` fails as if offline, `opencode2` fails as with no model unless seeded); `home/`, the scratch HOME with a `.gitconfig` naming `Verify Nico <verify-nico@example.com>` and a `config.toml` setting a free `port`, `tick_minutes = 600`, `bucket.path` to `bucket/` in the run dir and `morning.enabled = false`; `fixture/fixture` and `fixture/second`, clones of `verify/fixture` and `verify/second` whose GitHub origin URLs the scratch `.gitconfig` rewrites to bare repos on disk (any other github.com URL to a path that does not exist); serve started with `setsid`, ticking once at start with no Repo and then not for 10 hours, so every later tick is a `refresh` or the page's Refresh.

## Stable handles

CLI: the command words and flags (`--path`, `--json`, `--harness`, `--model`, `--effort`, `--sha`, `--findings`), the exit codes (`0` an answer, `1` a refusal as `{error, help}`, `2` a bad invocation), the JSON field names, and the routes in `GET` of an unknown path's `help`.

The Board (`/`), by ARIA role and name:

- `tablist "Projects"`: a `tab` per Project (a Repo with none is its own, named by its slug), then `tab "Libraries"`. Its `tabpanel "Libraries"`: `list "Libraries"` with `button "Remove <id>…"` per version, then `region "Add a Library"` with `textbox "Library"`, `textbox "Tag"`, `textbox "Subfolder"`, `button "Add"`.
- `region "Needs you"`, `button "Refresh"`.
- `banner`: `group "GitHub rate limits"` with `button "GitHub rate limits: <N>% of the tightest budget used"` (or `"GitHub paused until HH:MM"`), opening `heading "GitHub rate limits"` and a `heading "You · gh"` / `"Bot · ghbot"` per identity, each with `term` `REST` and `GraphQL`.
- A Project's tab: `region "<project>"` of its defaults (`region "Room"`, `"Switches"`, `"Settings"`) unless it is a Repo's own; then a `region "<slug>"` per Repo holding, in order, `region "Dispatch"` with `button "Dispatch"`, then `region "Readiness"`, `"Room"`, `"Switches"`, `"Settings"`, `"Libraries"`, `"Install"`, each folded to a `group` reading its head and what is set (`Readiness 2 of 6 pass`, `Room 3 · 0 in flight`, `Switches 4 of 10 on`, `Settings Implementer claude`, `Libraries None linked`, `Install None`) until `--click "region=<Section>"` opens it (a failing check or a refusal opens it by itself); then `button "Remove <slug>…"`. A `--click` takes the first match, the Project's defaults before any Repo's: use `--within "region=<slug>"`.
- Opened sections: `region "Room"` has `spinbutton "Room"`; `region "Switches"` a `switch` per switch (`Dispatch`, `Faux-nico answers`, `Flip to ready`, `Gardening`, `Only issues assigned to me`, `Quality-scan`, `Restart`, `Review`, `Stack`, `Wiki`); `region "Settings"` `group "Implementer"` and `group "Reviewer"` (`combobox "<Role> Harness"`, then `combobox` or, where the Harness lists none, `textbox` `"<Role> model"` and `"<Role> Effort"`), `textbox "Base"` and `combobox "Project"` (a Repo's only); `region "Libraries"` `button "Unlink <id>"` per version and `form "Link a Library"` (`textbox "Library"`, `"Tag"`, `"Subfolder"`, `button "Link"`); `region "Install"` `textbox "Install command"`. Beside each setting: `text: from Project` or `text: default` while it is taken, else `text: set here` and its × `button "Use the default <Setting>"` (or `"Use the Project's <Setting>"`); for two seconds after a save, `status: Saved` in their place.
- `navigation "View"` with `link "Map"` and `link "Wiki"`. The Wiki view: `button "Wiki settings"` opening `region "Settings for every Wiki"` with `combobox "Harness"`, `combobox` or `textbox` `"Model"` and `"Effort"`, `spinbutton "Hour"`, `"Calls"`, `"Depth"`, `"Calls at once"`, `textbox "Embedding model"`; then `list "Wikis"` with a `link "<id>"` per Wiki. The Map view: `region "Map of <project>"`.
- `region "Add a Repo"` with `textbox "Repo"`, `textbox "Its clone"`, `combobox "Its Project"`, `button "Add"`.

A Wiki's page (`--path /wiki/<id>`): `banner` with `link "Board"`, the id, its build and `button "Update"`, `button "Rebuild"` and, while the budget or a failed call left Gaps, `button "Continue +N calls"`; `searchbox "Search this Wiki"` with, while it holds words, a `status` `N found` and `navigation "Found"` with a `link` per hit in place of `navigation "Components"` with a `link` per Component; `article "<Component>"` with `navigation "Trail"`, `heading` level 1, a `region` per section it has (`"Terms"`, `"Interpretations"`, `"Relationships"`, `"Explainer"`, `"Components"`, `"Gaps"`) and `link "Next <name>"`; `navigation "Outline"` with a `button` per head; under 1080px `button "Outline"`, under 720px `button "Components"` too, each opening its sheet with `button "Close"`. The Wiki's page has no theme switch: `--scheme dark` is how it goes dark.
